🔄 Signal Processing Flow

1
ADC Input
adc_data[11:0] + adc_valid → Raw 12-bit samples from sensor (0-4095)
2
Noise Filtering (FIR)
8-tap moving average: output = (sum of 8 samples) / 8 → Removes high-frequency noise
3
Peak Detection
Track running_max (systolic) and running_min (diastolic) over 1200 samples
4
Heart Rate Calculation
Count threshold crossings → HR = peak_count × 5 (for 12-sec window)
5
ADC to mmHg Conversion
bp_mmhg = (adc × 67) >> 10 → Maps ADC values to blood pressure
6
Alert Generation
HIGH: sys≥140 OR dia≥90 | LOW: sys≤90 OR dia≤60
